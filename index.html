What this is. Multi-frame super-resolution: register every frame against the first (phase correlation, then Lucas–Kanade for shift and a small rotation), keep the last 16 in a ring, and on each new frame visit four of them and add back frame − shrink(warp(estimate)). The start is SihtiSR on the first frame. The same idea is Irani & Peleg (1991), and Google's Pixel phones do it with hand tremor (Wronski et al., 2019). This is a small, readable version. Limits: it assumes the scene is still and the camera moves rigidly; moving objects smear. A camera on a desk does not shake, so pick the phone up. Phone video is already sharpened and compressed, so real gains are smaller than in the demo, where the numbers above are measured against the true image. Code, benchmark and tests.
